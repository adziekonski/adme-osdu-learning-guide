# ADME and OSDU Learning Guide

This is a guide I wrote while learning **Azure Data Manager for Energy (ADME)**
and the **OSDU Data Platform** it runs, starting from zero. It explains things
the way I came to understand them, so it's **subjective**: what I found
important, the order that made sense to me, and the simplifications that
helped.

The focus is on **understanding** ADME and OSDU, and how to **use** them: what
they are, why organisations use them, what goes inside and what doesn't, and how
data and applications work with them. It's written for data, AI and software
engineers, architects and consultants who know Azure but have no oil and gas
background.

**Start here: [docs/README.md](docs/README.md)** for the sections and the
reading path.

> **Independent learning notes.** This guide isn't official Microsoft
> documentation and isn't supported by Microsoft. ADME and OSDU change over
> time, so docs about changing facts state when those facts were checked.
> Always confirm against
> [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/energy-data-services/) and
> the [OSDU community documentation](https://community.opengroup.org/osdu).
